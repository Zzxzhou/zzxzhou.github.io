Own-Cluster Reuse Bias in Orthogonal Panel Estimation

Working Paper
[ Abstract ]

Orthogonal scores remove first-order sensitivity to globally estimated nuisance functions, but not necessarily bias from a unit coordinate estimated and evaluated with the same dependent cluster. We call this own-cluster reuse. In a partially linear panel model, an exact fixed-sieve expansion separates two drift terms of order $T^{-1}$: derivative–influence covariance and curvature. Either can shift the $\sqrt{NT}$ limit when $N/T$ approaches a positive constant. For a sample-mean coordinate and growing tensor-product spline sieve, we estimate both long-run terms while retaining the full unit history and construct a cluster long-run variance estimator. Under independent units, deterministic quasi-uniform coordinates, and bounded fixed-range within-unit dependence, the correction restores asymptotic linearity and standard-normal studentization. Buffered within-unit splitting removes covariance but leaves—and under an equal split doubles—curvature. In a nonlinear $N = T = 200$ design, correction reduces $\sqrt{NT}$-scaled bias from $0.744$ to $0.008$ and yields 95.9% coverage.
